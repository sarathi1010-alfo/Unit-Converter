# WEEKLY CONTENT ENGINE: UNITFLOW EDITION

This document outlines the repeatable weekly strategy for building massive semantic surface area for UnitFlow.

## 1. THE REPEATABLE WEEKLY TEMPLATE SYSTEM

### Template A – Cluster Article (700-1000 words)
**Goal:** Deep educational authority and internal linking.
- **H1:** `[PRIMARY_KEYWORD]`: Complete Guide for `[TARGET_AUDIENCE]` in 2026
- **AI Snapshot:** 30-word intro definition for Google AI Overview/Featured Snippets.
- **H2:** Why `[PRIMARY_KEYWORD]` Matters for `[AUDIENCE/USE_CASE]`
- **H2:** How to `[ACTION]` with `[PRIMARY_KEYWORD]` (step-by-step)
- **H2:** Top `[NUMBER]` `[STRATEGIES/TOOLS]` for `[PRIMARY_KEYWORD]`
- **H3:** Detailed breakdown of each (with real examples)
- **H2:** Common Mistakes to Avoid (with table)
- **AEO Box:** 5 Key Takeaways (bullet points)
- **AEO Box:** FAQ Section with 5 questions + `FAQPage` Schema
- **Internal Links:** 1 Pillar + 2 Clusters + Converter Tool

### Template B – Programmatic Conversion Guide Page (400-600 words)
**Goal:** Infinite scaling for long-tail queries.
- **H1:** `[UNIT_A]` to `[UNIT_B]` Converter: Quick & Accurate `[CATEGORY]` Conversion
- **Quick Answer:** 1 `[UNIT_A]` = `[FACTOR]` `[UNIT_B]` + mathematical formula.
- **Conversion Table:** 10+ rows of common values (e.g., 1, 5, 10, 20, 50, 100, 500, 1000).
- **H2:** How to Convert `[UNIT_A]` to `[UNIT_B]` (step-by-step + formula)
- **H2:** Real-World Examples of `[UNIT_A]` to `[UNIT_B]` Conversion (e.g., in cooking or engineering)
- **AEO Box:** "When to Use `[UNIT_A]` vs `[UNIT_B]`" (Visual comparison or context)
- **Internal Links:** Category Page + Tool + Related Conversions

### Template C – Micro-Answer Page (300-500 words)
**Goal:** Featured snippets and voice search optimization.
- **H1:** What is `[UNIT_TERM]`? (e.g., "What is a Newton?")
- **AI Snapshot:** 40-60 word definition.
- **H2:** How Does `[TERM]` Work?
- **H2:** Real-World Example of `[TERM]` in `[INDUSTRY]`
- **H2:** Why is `[TERM]` Important?
- **H2:** `[TERM]` vs `[RELATED_TERM]` (comparison table)
- **AEO Box:** Core Principles (bullet points)
- **Internal Links:** 1 Cluster + Tool

### Template D – Pillar Page (3000-7000 words)
**Goal:** Weekly hub for a broad category.
- **H1:** The Ultimate 2026 Guide to `[BROAD_TOPIC]` (e.g., "Guide to Length Conversion")
- **Executive Summary:** 200 words for AI Overviews.
- **Table of Contents:** Jump links to chapters.
- **Chapters:** Introduction/Evolution, Technical Foundation, Top 10 Strategies, Case Study, Future Trends.
- **AEO Box:** Statistical Data Table.
- **AEO Box:** 10+ FAQ with Schema.
- **Internal Links:** 15+ Cluster Articles + Tool.

### Template E – Use-Case Page (600-800 words)
**Goal:** Targeting specific intent (Commercial/Informational).
- **H1:** Unit Converter for `[USE_CASE]`: The Complete 2026 Guide (e.g., "Unit Converter for Cooking")
- **H2:** Why unit conversion matters in `[USE_CASE]`
- **H2:** Top 5 most common conversions for `[USE_CASE]`
- **H2:** How to use UnitFlow for `[USE_CASE]` (step-by-step)
- **AEO Box:** Quick Reference Table (Use Case → Common Conversions → Tips)
- **Internal Links:** Pillar + Tool + Category

### Template F – Comparison Page (500-700 words)
**Goal:** Capturing competitor-branded traffic.
- **H1:** `[ENTITY_A]` vs `[ENTITY_B]`: Which Unit Converter is Better for `[USE_CASE]`?
- **Quick Verdict:** 1-sentence AI summary ("UnitFlow wins for keyboard-speed, Google wins for zero-click math").
- **Comparison Table:** (Features, UnitFlow, `[COMPETITOR]`, Winner).
- **H2:** Deep Dive UnitFlow (Pros/Cons)
- **H2:** Deep Dive `[COMPETITOR]` (Pros/Cons)
- **H2:** When to choose each
- **AEO Box:** "Best Pick for [Scenario]"
- **Internal Links:** Pillar + Tool
