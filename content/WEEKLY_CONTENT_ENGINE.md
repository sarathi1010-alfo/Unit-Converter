# UnitFlow: Weekly Content Engine (SEO Strategy 2026)

This document outlines the repeatable, scalable content strategy for building massive semantic surface area for UnitFlow.

## 1. THE REPEATABLE TEMPLATE SYSTEM

### Template A – Cluster Article (700-1000 words)
**Goal:** Educational depth and authority on technical concepts.

- **H1:** `[PRIMARY_KEYWORD]`: Complete Guide for `[TARGET_AUDIENCE]` in 2026
  - *Example: "Imperial vs Metric Systems: Complete Guide for Engineering Students in 2026"*
- **AI Summary (30 words):** High-level definition for Google AI Overview.
- **H2:** Why `[PRIMARY_KEYWORD]` Matters for `[AUDIENCE/USE_CASE]`
- **H2:** How to `[ACTION]` with `[PRIMARY_KEYWORD]` (step-by-step)
- **H2:** Top `[NUMBER]` `[STRATEGIES/TOOLS]` for `[PRIMARY_KEYWORD]`
- **H3:** Detailed breakdown of each (with real examples)
- **H2:** Common Mistakes to Avoid (with table)
- **AEO Box (`AICitationBlock`):** 5 Key Takeaways (bullets)
- **FAQ Section:** 5 questions + FAQPage Schema.
- **Schema:** `Article`, `FAQPage`.
- **Internal Links:** 1 Pillar + 2 Clusters + Converter Tool.

---

### Template B – Programmatic Conversion Guide Page (400-600 words)
**Goal:** Capture high-volume "unit to unit" search intent.

- **H1:** `[UNIT_A]` to `[UNIT_B]` Converter: Quick & Accurate `[CATEGORY]` Conversion
  - *Example: "cm to inches Converter: Quick & Accurate Length Conversion"*
- **Quick Answer:** 1 `[UNIT_A]` = `[FACTOR]` `[UNIT_B]` + formula.
- **Conversion Table:** 10+ rows of common values.
- **H2:** How to Convert `[UNIT_A]` to `[UNIT_B]` (step-by-step + formula)
- **H2:** Real-World Examples of `[UNIT_A]` to `[UNIT_B]` Conversion
- **AEO Box (`AICitationBlock`):** "When to Use `[UNIT_A]` vs `[UNIT_B]`"
- **Schema:** `WebApplication` (the converter), `FAQPage`.
- **Internal Links:** Category Page + Tool + Related Conversions.

---

### Template C – Micro-Answer Page (300-500 words)
**Goal:** Featured snippets and voice search.

- **H1:** What is `[UNIT_TERM]`? (direct question format)
  - *Example: "What is a Conversion Factor?"*
- **Direct Answer (40-60 words):** Concise definition.
- **H2:** How Does `[TERM]` Work?
- **H2:** Real-World Example of `[TERM]` in `[INDUSTRY]`
- **H2:** Why is `[TERM]` Important?
- **H2:** `[TERM]` vs `[RELATED_TERM]` (comparison table)
- **AEO Box (`AICitationBlock`):** Core Principles (bullets).
- **Schema:** `Article` (targeted at Speakable property).
- **Internal Links:** 1 Cluster + Tool.

---

### Template D – Pillar Page (3000-7000 words)
**Goal:** Weekly category hubs and massive internal link power.

- **H1:** The Ultimate 2026 Guide to `[BROAD_TOPIC]`
  - *Example: "The Ultimate 2026 Guide to Length Conversion"*
- **Executive Summary:** 200 words for AI Overviews.
- **Table of Contents:** Jump links to chapters.
- **H2 Chapters:** Introduction/Evolution, Technical Foundation, Top 10 Strategies, Case Study, Future Trends.
- **AEO Box:** Statistical Data Table.
- **FAQ Section:** 10+ FAQ with Schema.
- **Schema:** `Article`, `FAQPage`.
- **Internal Links:** 15+ Cluster Articles + Tool.

---

### Template E – Use-Case Page (600-800 words)
**Goal:** Target specific audiences and commercial intent.

- **H1:** Unit Converter for `[USE_CASE]`: The Complete 2026 Guide
  - *Example: "Unit Converter for Cooking: The Complete 2026 Guide"*
- **H2:** Why unit conversion matters in `[USE_CASE]`
- **H2:** Top 5 most common conversions for `[USE_CASE]`
- **H2:** How to use UnitFlow for `[USE_CASE]` (step-by-step)
- **AEO Box (`AICitationBlock`):** Quick Reference Table (Use Case → Common Conversions → Tips).
- **Schema:** `Article`, `HowTo` (for the step-by-step tool usage).
- **Internal Links:** Pillar + Tool + Category.

---

### Template F – Comparison Page (500-700 words)
**Goal:** Target competitor brand names.

- **H1:** `[ENTITY_A]` vs `[ENTITY_B]`: Which Unit Converter is Better for `[USE_CASE]`?
  - *Example: "UnitFlow vs Google Converter: Which Unit Converter is Better for Professionals?"*
- **Quick Verdict:** 1-sentence AI summary.
- **Comparison Table:** (Features, `[ENTITY_A]`, `[ENTITY_B]`, Winner).
- **H2:** Deep Dive `[ENTITY_A]` (Pros/Cons)
- **H2:** Deep Dive `[ENTITY_B]` (Pros/Cons)
- **H2:** When to choose each.
- **AEO Box (`AICitationBlock`):** "Best Pick for [Scenario]".
- **Schema:** `Article`, `Review` (if applicable).
- **Internal Links:** Pillar + Tool.

## 3. THE ETERNAL WEEKLY RHYTHM (Cadence)

- **Weekly Pillar:** 1 per week (Every Wednesday).
- **Weekly Use-Case Pages:** 2–3 per week.
- **Weekly Comparisons:** 1–2 per week.
- **Daily Programmatic Guides:** 5–10 per day (Automated).
- **Daily Clusters:** 3–5 per day.
- **Daily Micro-Answers:** 5 per day.
- **TARGET:** 50–100 new indexable pages per week.
