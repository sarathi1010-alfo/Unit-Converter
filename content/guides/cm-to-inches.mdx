# How to Convert Centimeters to Inches

Converting centimeters (cm) to inches (in) is a common task in both everyday life and scientific fields. Here is a simple guide on how to perform this conversion.

## The Formula

The formula to convert centimeters to inches is straightforward:

**1 cm = 0.3937008 inches**

To convert a value from centimeters to inches, you can multiply the value by `0.3937008`, or equivalently, divide it by `2.54`.

### Example

If you want to convert 10 centimeters to inches:

* `10 cm × 0.3937008 = 3.937 inches`
* or `10 cm / 2.54 = 3.937 inches`
