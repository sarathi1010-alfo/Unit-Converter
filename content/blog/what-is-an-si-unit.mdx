## How Do SI Units Work?
The system comprises seven base units: the meter (length), kilogram (mass), second (time), ampere (electric current), kelvin (thermodynamic temperature), mole (amount of substance), and candela (luminous intensity). All other units are derived from these base units.

## Real-World Example in Global Trade
When trading commodities internationally, using the kilogram (an SI unit) guarantees that both the buyer and the seller agree precisely on the quantity being exchanged.

## SI Units vs Traditional Metric
While closely related, SI units are a more modernized, strict standardization of the original metric system, specifically redefining units based on immutable physical constants rather than physical artifacts.
