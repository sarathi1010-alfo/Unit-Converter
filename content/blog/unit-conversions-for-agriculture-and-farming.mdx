## Land Area: Acres and Hectares

The most common conversion in global agriculture is between acres (imperial) and hectares (metric). Depending on where you purchase equipment or seed, you may need to switch between the two.

### Converting Hectares to Acres
The standard conversion is: **1 hectare = 2.47105 acres**
- **Mental Hack:** To quickly estimate hectares to acres in the field, simply multiply by 2.5. If you have 100 hectares, you have roughly 250 acres. (The exact amount is 247.1 acres).

### Converting Acres to Square Feet and Meters
When planning small plots or greenhouses, you need smaller units:
- **1 Acre = 43,560 square feet.**
- **1 Hectare = 10,000 square meters.**

## Liquid Volume: Gallons and Liters

Proper irrigation and chemical application require precise volume conversions. In the US, application rates are often measured in gallons per acre, while international equipment may use liters per hectare.

### Gallons to Liters
The conversion is: **1 US Gallon = 3.78541 Liters**
- **Mental Hack:** A gallon is roughly 4 liters. If you need 50 gallons of water for a tank mix, that is approximately 200 liters (exactly 189.27 liters).

### Application Rates: Gallons/Acre to Liters/Hectare
To convert an application rate from Gallons per Acre (GPA) to Liters per Hectare (L/ha), multiply the GPA by **9.354**.
- *Example:* 10 GPA × 9.354 = 93.54 L/ha.

## Weight and Mass: Crop Yields

When harvesting, crop yields are measured in tons, pounds, or kilograms. Understanding the difference between a metric ton and a US ton is critical when selling commodities.

- **1 US Ton (Short Ton) = 2,000 pounds (lbs)**
- **1 Metric Ton (Tonne) = 1,000 kilograms (kg) ≈ 2,204.62 pounds**

Always confirm which "ton" a buyer is referring to, as the 200-pound difference can significantly impact your bottom line on large contracts.
