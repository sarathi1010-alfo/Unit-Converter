# What is a Conversion Factor?

## How Does a Conversion Factor Work?
By multiplying an initial measurement by a conversion factor, you cancel out the original unit and introduce the desired unit. For instance, to convert 10 inches to centimeters, you multiply by 2.54 cm/inch. The 'inches' cancel out, leaving 25.4 cm.

## Real-World Example in Engineering
Engineers constantly use conversion factors when translating schematics from metric (millimeters) to imperial (inches) to ensure components manufactured globally fit together perfectly.

## Conversion Factor vs Conversion Formula
| Feature | Conversion Factor | Conversion Formula |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Nature | A constant multiplier | A mathematical equation |
| Usage | Linear conversions (e.g. length, weight) | Non-linear conversions (e.g. temperature) |
| Example | 1 inch = 2.54 cm | F = (C * 9/5) + 32 |
