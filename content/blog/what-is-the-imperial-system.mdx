## How Does the Imperial System Work?
Unlike the metric system, imperial units do not scale by powers of 10. For instance, there are 12 inches in a foot, 3 feet in a yard, and 5,280 feet in a mile.

## Real-World Example in Construction
In the United States, residential construction relies almost entirely on the imperial system. Lumber is sold in feet and inches (e.g., a 2x4).
